WHO WE ARE

Football Is More Than the Game.

The Union of Liberian Footballers (ULF) is a not-for-profit organization established to promote the shared interests, dignity, welfare, and professional growth of people involved in Liberian football.

OUR PURPOSE

Building a Stronger Future for Football

Players' Rights

Promoting and protecting the rights and professional interests of current and former footballers.

Welfare & Support

Advancing member welfare, including social, medical, educational, and emergency support objectives.

Representation

Representing members' interests in engagement with football institutions and relevant authorities.

Growth & Integrity

Encouraging professional development, grassroots football, accountability, and good governance.
